# Surface brief: home (`app/page.tsx`)

Scope: the single-page bilingual portfolio. Mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: Saudi recruiters and hiring managers for finance-transformation, applied-AI and ERP roles; decide within seconds whether to contact. Action: email / phone / LinkedIn, or download the CV. Proof: resume figures, repository-verified product facts, honest status. Constraints: PRODUCT.md guardrails; light theme; EN/AR with RTL.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The career filed as a Saudi bilingual annual report: a cover, key figures, colour-coded segments, and numbered notes holding the proof behind each claim. Refuses the dark developer hero with skill badges and card grids, and the cream-and-serif editorial page.

OWN-WORLD: Bright white report stock, ink #12151C, hairline column rules. Three flat segment inks own whole regions: ledger green (Finance & ERP), cobalt (Engineering), saffron (Applied AI); ink stands for Delivery. Square corners, 1.5px ink borders, tabular figures, numbered note markers. Depth only from overlapping flat sheets; no shadows, gradients, glass or blur. Archivo (wide display cut) with Readex Pro for Arabic.

STORY: Visitors learn he spans finance, ERP, engineering and AI; believe it through figures, real system topologies and plainly stated status; then they email, call or download the CV.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Masthead with bilingual name, numbered contents and CV button. Cover: the name at monumental wide scale, "Finance & AI Transformation" beneath, intro and actions in the right column, then a full-width career-by-segment chart (2018 to now) where three lanes converge in 2024–2026.

FORM: Grounded candidate 3 of 7 (bilingual annual report with notes), seed 28eb0419. Raises: ghost rows show what is not built (seven-segment); overlap-only depth (papercut); one winning figure per spread (fly-posting); products shown through real topology (algorave); every diagram part named (mook). Signature interaction: note markers open a flat note sheet linked to the source entry. Motion grammar: entries post in chronological order, once.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
